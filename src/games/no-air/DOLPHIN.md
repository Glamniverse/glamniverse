# NO AIR M3 — original dolphin encounter

One original procedural bottlenose dolphin; no third-party animal assets, textures, clips or dependencies. Authored elliptical body sections form the melon, rostrum, torso and narrowing caudal peduncle. Swept solid foils form the dorsal fin, paired pectoral flippers and horizontal flukes. Small eyes, mouth lines and a blowhole share the merged geometry and material. Blue-gray dorsal vertex colours grade into a lighter underside.

Budget: one opaque draw per view, 7,252 triangles, one material, zero textures/lights/shadows/transparency. Vertex animation bends the rear body vertically and adds slight pectoral motion; the head is stable. Approximate length 3.45 m including swept flukes. Shared world disposal owns GPU resources; disposal freezes the dolphin update. No controller listeners, interaction targets or audio.

A 190-second arc-length route crosses open water from the canyon toward the shelf, then departs into deep blue. Phases: distant travel 0–40s, scenic pass 40–75s, optional curious adjustment 75–94s, departure 94–130s, distant roaming 130–190s. Only the curious interval allows a small, smoothly enveloped target adjustment toward a viewer 7–15m away. No chasing. Local steering redirects targets within 7m of the viewer and brakes inward travel progressively; tested stationary viewers retain >4.5m centre clearance. Speed <=1.25m/s, acceleration <=.55m/s², turn rate <=.5rad/s, bank <=.22rad. Tracking gaps/long frames freeze simulation time rather than advancing the route suddenly. Headset teleportation or a user deliberately pursuing an animal cannot be guaranteed collision-free by soft steering; no forced player displacement is used.

The authored route clears terrain and all 72 existing rock instances with a conservative 1.8m creature margin. Viewer avoidance is lightweight local steering, not mesh collision/pathfinding. Quest must judge the real encounter, silhouette, fluke motion, clearances, existing-life coexistence and frame stability. No Quest performance claim is made before physical testing.

Existing ocean, life, locomotion, song, menu, Left Y and Exit implementations are unchanged. Integration adds only create/update/dispose calls in the NO AIR entry module. Local review HTML/screenshots are ignored development artifacts and are not deployed. Preview entry remains the existing NO AIR card. No Production release is authorized.
